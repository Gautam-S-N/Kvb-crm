# KVB CRM - Complete Features Overview

The KVB CRM is an enterprise-grade ERP and CRM system designed to handle every aspect of a company's sales pipeline, internal operations, inventory, and employee tracking. Below is the exhaustive list of every feature currently supported by the platform.

## 1. User & Organizational Management
*   **Hierarchical RBAC (Role-Based Access Control):** Four built-in roles (`SUPER_ADMIN`, `ADMIN`, `MANAGER`, `EMPLOYEE`). Permissions can be toggled per-user independently of their role (e.g., granting an employee `canAssignLeads`).
*   **Materialized Path Subordination:** The system supports infinite depth for management structures. A manager can instantly view the data of their direct subordinates, and all of the subordinates underneath them.
*   **Suspension Handover Guard:** An admin cannot suspend a manager who oversees active employees without reassigning their team first, preventing orphaned accounts.
*   **Delegated Authority:** Managers going on leave can temporarily delegate their management abilities to a peer.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
*   **Central Customer Directory:** Maintains an exhaustive database of all client interactions, contact details, and social tracking (Facebook PSID).
*   **Advanced Lead Tracking:** Leads flow through distinct states (`NEW`, `INQUIRY`, `NEGOTIATING`, `WON`, `LOST`). 
*   **Lead Timelines & Activity Logs:** Every status change, note, email, and WhatsApp message sent is permanently recorded in the lead's historical timeline.
*   **Smart Overdue Escalation:** Background systems actively monitor the pipeline and alert management if an employee neglects a lead for over 7 days.
*   **Follow-Up Scheduling:** Employees can schedule specific follow-up times with clients and receive push notifications when the call is due.

## 3. Sales & Financial Engine
*   **Product Catalog Management:** Maintain a centralized list of products with base prices, categories, and specific HSN/SAC tax codes.
*   **Dynamic Quotation Builder:** Sales reps can draft customized quotations containing both catalog products and on-the-fly custom line items. Applies variable discounts and computes GST/tax automatically.
*   **Race-Condition Locking:** Quotation numbers are reserved per-user to prevent numbering collisions when multiple agents draft quotes simultaneously.
*   **Quotation to Sale Conversion:** Approved quotes convert to `Sale` records with a single click, tracking `Payment Status` (Unpaid, Partial, Paid) and `Delivery Status`.
*   **Automated PDF Generation:** Both Quotations and Sales instantly generate formatted, brand-compliant PDF documents.

## 4. Procurement & Inventory
*   **Vendor Directory:** Tracks all external suppliers, their GST numbers, and payment terms.
*   **Purchase Item Master:** A separate catalog specifically for procurement goods and raw materials.
*   **Purchase Order (PO) Management:** Generate multi-item purchase orders, calculate sub-totals and taxes, and export as PDFs for vendors. Tracks POs from `DRAFT` to `RECEIVED`.
*   **Raw Material Inventory:** Live tracking of stock levels with complete historical logs of stock check-ins and check-outs.

## 5. Employee Field Operations
*   **Tasks & Checklists:** Assign discrete, priority-based tasks (`LOW` to `URGENT`) to employees. Tasks support sub-item checklists.
*   **Proof of Execution:** Employees can attach photographic evidence or record direct audio voice-notes from their phone to prove task completion.
*   **Daily Check-In Reports:** At shift's end, employees submit summaries of their work.
*   **Employee Task Tracking:** Managers have a dedicated **Employee Tracking** dashboard that visualizes task completion rates, overdue tasks, and general productivity metrics across their entire team using live charts.
*   **Personal To-Do Lists:** A private, kanban-style to-do list for individual employee productivity, decoupled from assigned tasks.

## 6. Goal Tracking & Automation
*   **Sales Target System:** Management sets revenue, lead, and quotation goals (`WEEKLY`, `MONTHLY`, `QUARTERLY`, `YEARLY`).
*   **Target Roll-Ups:** A manager's performance goal is achieved by summing their direct sales plus the revenue generated by their entire subordinate team.
*   **Automated Target Reminders:** The system celebrates milestones (e.g., "50% Target Hit") and warns employees via notifications as deadlines approach.
